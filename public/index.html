The Farm

Raising cattle since 1886.

The Balvanz family has been raising cattle in America since 1886. More than 140 years later, we're still at it in Iowa. Our cows raise their calves on pasture, and family looks after them every day: checking the herd, putting up hay, and handling cattle the right way.

We keep things simple: healthy cattle, honest dealings, and a handshake that means something. If you have questions about our cattle, we'd be glad to hear from you.

Certified

Beef Quality Assurance certified

Our farm is BQA certified, which means we follow the national Beef Quality Assurance guidelines for animal care, low-stress handling, herd health, and record keeping.
